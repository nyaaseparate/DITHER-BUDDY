How it works

1. Load anything. 2. Pick one of 64 algorithms on the right — error diffusion, ordered Bayer, patterns, glitch slices, halftones, ASCII, stitch. 3. Recolor with a built-in palette, your own custom colors, or auto-extracted colors from the image. 4.
